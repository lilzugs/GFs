## 3. Posterior assessment from the user

The review ranked view 4 first because its waist, hip breadth, posterior silhouette and upper-thigh separation work together on a comparatively slim underlying frame. The small diamond-shaped negative-space/shadow relationship at the upper leg junction was specifically noted as a useful visual landmark. View 2 was ranked second for its slim thighs and transitions, although the posterior appears slightly high-set. View 3 was ranked third for its convincing weight and side profile. View 5 was ranked last; it should not override the other references on posterior form.

These are **reference evaluations**, not quantified sculpting measurements or a mandate to modify the approved archival images. See the existing rear-three-quarter and side-posterior guides for historical checkpoints.

- Black collar, sleeve cuffs, underbust/ribcage band and knee bands: **#111111**.
- Warm-gold O-ring as established.

- Temporary palettes and historical rendering artifacts are not geometry specifications.

## 6. Turnaround failure analysis and continuation

Experimental five-view generations `01cc3763-aafd-40d1-9b74-9b2e70ece3ae`, `94596af3-e932-4d56-bed0-6bc86374308d`, `e16c3240-1bc2-4d4e-8a7b-78c71e743608`, `6e409548-693a-4217-ba6c-57221e4da735` and `846ee594-8eec-495a-9018-28e71bba891f` were exploratory, not canonical. They introduced inconsistent proportions, invented neckline shapes, pupil/iris drift, incorrect view direction, wider thighs and uneven rendering styles.

Preferred continuation: reconcile individual views before arranging a new presentation sheet, and use the actual reference artwork and this regional decision table for quality control. Preserve source images unchanged. Present the shared head-to-knee range for comparisons when full-body data is not available; do not invent feet or force anatomy into alignment by nonuniform stretching. Use visual comparisons for design guidance rather than implying the independent illustrations are a measured 3D mesh.

Retain the individually approved source checkpoints even when a later consolidated sheet is visually appealing. A new candidate is not canonical until approved by the user.
