## Palettes and appearance

The exact currently published working swatches are in index.html, with per-character data-type, data-reason and data-palette-status attributes. Working swatches are not final approved render palettes. Do not invent precise replacement hex values from verbal colour descriptions.

Hair may use two tones: a main colour plus a subtle highlight or deliberate contrasting streak. Hair rules are species dependent; fin structures and hairless characters need individual treatment. Fur/skin hybrids such as Bibi and Vesper use a separate natural human skin tone beneath their fur. Loveander requires distinct skin and scale tones. These material colours are separate from the three UI swatches.

## Workshop behaviour

reasons.html seeds the shared alpha Reason assignments on a fresh browser. Existing local browser selections take precedence, including deliberately cleared assignments. Export local changes to share them; browser storage does not update GitHub.
