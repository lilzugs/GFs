## Iteration and correction

The initial profile retained a three-quarter neckline and visible far-side contour. The approved correction uses true lateral occlusion: the near breast hides the far breast in this symmetrical stylized model. This is a viewing-angle correction, not removal of anatomy or reduction of volume.

## Locked visual criteria

- One uninterrupted outer chest silhouette with no doubled contour or visible central cleavage.
- Preserve the approved forward projection, soft upper slope, rounded lower contour and gravity-set shape.
- The neckline follows the side perspective; fabric tension follows the single visible contour.
- Preserve its relationship to the narrow ribcage, black underbust trim and white garment.
- Keep the source's neutral right-facing profile, gray sculpting-clay palette, and clean cartoon rendering style.
- Do not derive exact 3D measurements from the illustration; the approved contour is the visual standard.
